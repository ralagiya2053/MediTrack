          cover third-party sites linked from MediTrack.
        - State that MediTrack is a demo/learning project.

     2. Information We Collect
        - Account data: name, email, hashed password.
        - Health data: symptoms, severity, notes, and any vitals the

        - Technical data: IP address, browser type, timestamps.
        - Explicitly state we do NOT collect payment info (no payments

        - To authenticate you.
        - To improve the service in aggregate.
        - We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT use health data for

          special category and processed on the basis of your explicit
          consent, which you give by creating an account and logging
          entries.

     5. How We Store and Protect Your Data
        - Data is stored in a database on the server hosting MediTrack.
        - Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text).
        - We take reasonable technical measures but no system is 100%
          secure — say this plainly.

     6. Sharing and Disclosure
        - We do not share your data with third parties except:
          (a) when legally required, (b) with your explicit direction
          (e.g. exporting a report to share with your doctor).
        - If third-party services are added later (e.g. wearable APIs),
          this policy will be updated before that happens.

     7. Your Rights
        - Access: you can view all your data in the app.
        - Correction: you can edit any log entry.
        - Deletion: you can delete individual entries; account deletion
          removes all associated data.
        - Portability: you may export your data (feature planned).
        - Contact us to exercise any of these.

     8. Data Retention
        - Data is retained while your account is active.
        - On account deletion, personal data is removed from active
          systems; backups may persist briefly.

     9. Children's Privacy
        - MediTrack is not intended for users under 18 without guardian
          consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

     10. Cookies and Sessions
         - We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in.
         - No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

     11. Changes to This Policy
         - We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will
           reflect changes.
         - Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

     12. Contact
         - Placeholder email: privacy@meditrack.example
